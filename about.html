ABOUT US

At Verolite, we believe exceptional lighting does more than illuminate a space—it enhances architecture, elevates interiors, and enriches everyday experiences. As a UK-based lighting brand, we are committed to delivering premium LED lighting solutions that combine innovative technology, outstanding performance, and contemporary design.

Our comprehensive product portfolio includes recessed and surface-mounted LED downlights, magnetic track lighting systems, linear lighting, LED strip lighting, office luminaires, outdoor lighting, and flexible neon solutions. Every product is thoughtfully engineered using high-quality materials, including precision die-cast aluminium, and incorporates reliable components from globally recognized technology partners such as Osram, Philips, Bridgelux, and Cree.

Quality, efficiency, and reliability are at the core of everything we create. Our lighting solutions are designed to deliver superior colour rendering, exceptional energy efficiency, long operational lifespans of up to 50,000 hours, and robust protection ratings for demanding indoor and outdoor environments. Each product is developed to meet the evolving requirements of modern residential, commercial, hospitality, and architectural lighting projects.

Whether you are designing a luxury residence, specifying lighting for a commercial development, or delivering a large-scale architectural project, Verolite provides lighting solutions that seamlessly combine performance, durability, and elegant design.
